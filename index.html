Analytics projects

Testing a fan-growth assumption with NLP

New England Revolution × Hult Applied Consulting · R · Summer 2026 · repo

  • Built an NLP pipeline over 6,572 public fan documents using sentiment lexicons, LDA topic modeling, and TF-IDF.
  • Tested, and disproved, the client's assumption that the 2026 World Cup was driving fan conversation: a 0.7% mention rate.
  • Located the real problem in service touchpoints, not match content: app store sentiment 65% negative and ticketing 78% negative against positive match-day content.

Route optimization and the case for a $299 fee

RH × Hult BI Capstone · Python (OR-Tools), Power BI · Spring 2026 · repo

  • Replaced a manual routing and costing process with a cost-minimization model across 19 white-glove customer orders, cutting modelled delivery emissions 9% (674 to 614 kg).
  • Delivered a 6-page Power BI dashboard (loading manifest, route visualization, cost summary, CO2 tracking) on a live SharePoint pipeline with Power Query and DAX.
  • Recommended RH keep its $299 delivery fee as a brand anchor, backed by a $650.09 average cost-per-order analysis. Sometimes the right recommendation is to change nothing.

Churn prediction where the cheaper model won

UK online retailer dataset · R · Spring 2026 · repo

  • Built a churn model on 805K retail transactions (5,038 customers) with RFM features and temporal validation: 74.3% sensitivity, 72.4% accuracy, 0.797 AUC.
  • Chose logistic regression over a higher-accuracy decision tree because a churner-detection cost analysis favored it, and shipped an executive brief with a monthly scoring plan and re-engagement triggers.

Predicting urban heat islands with LightGBM

Gradient boosting, SHAP interpretability · repo

  • Modelled urban heat island intensity with LightGBM and used SHAP to make the drivers legible to non-technical readers.
  • The team model scored 0.87 F1 against independent ground truth; my solo variant reached 0.92 F1 under cross-validation (not ground-truth scored, and I say so, because the difference matters).
